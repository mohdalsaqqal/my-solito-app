
# Home Page Structure Specification

## Purpose
To serve as the primary storefront vitrine, introducing users to key brand partnerships, promotional offers, and curated product selections. It balances editorial storytelling with direct commerce opportunities.

## Section Sequence

### 1. Hero Slider
- **Purpose**: High-impact visual introduction to major brands and current campaigns.
- **Elements**:
  - **Background Media**: Lifestyle imagery setting the mood.
  - **Foreground Media**: Isolated product imagery to show specific offerings.
  - **Brand Heading**: Large typography identifying the featured brand.
  - **Subtitle**: Contextual tag (e.g., "Dermatological Laboratory").
  - **CTA Button**: "Shop Now" action leading to specific collections.

### 2. Announcement Marquee
- **Purpose**: Communicates value propositions and logistics information continuously.
- **Elements**:
  - **Scrolling Text**: Short phrases separated by dividers (e.g., Shipping info, Welcome offers).

### 3. Best Sellers Carousel
- **Purpose**: Immediate product discovery based on popularity/seasonality.
- **Elements**:
  - **Section Header**: Title and navigation controls.
  - **Product Cards**: Individual items with price, rating, and quick actions.

### 4. Bundle & Save Carousel
- **Purpose**: Promotes higher average order value (AOV) through curated sets.
- **Elements**:
  - **Section Header**: Title.
  - **Bundle Cards**: Larger cards listing included items, savings calculation, and direct "Add Bundle" action.

### 5. Featured Brand Banner (Editorial)
- **Purpose**: A static, immersive banner highlighting a specific partnership.
- **Elements**:
  - **Background Image**: Full-width aesthetic visual.
  - **Overlay Text**: Heading and descriptive copy.
  - **CTA Button**: Link to the brand's collection page.

### 6. Brand Spotlight A (Split Layout)
- **Purpose**: Educational storytelling for a specific dermatological brand (e.g., CeraVe).
- **Elements**:
  - **Text Column**: Brand logo/name, headline, descriptive paragraph, link to brand page.
  - **Image Column**: Lifestyle or clinical photography.

### 7. Brand Spotlight A Products
- **Purpose**: Direct commerce follow-up to the storytelling section above.
- **Elements**:
  - **Filtered Product List**: Carousel of products belonging exclusively to Brand A.

### 8. Brand Spotlight B Container
- **Purpose**: Visual grouping for a second brand highlight (e.g., SVR), distinct from the white background.
- **Elements**:
  - **Background Wrapper**: Soft colored background to delineate the section.
  - **Split Layout (Reversed)**: Image on left, Text on right (desktop).
  - **Filtered Product List**: Carousel of products belonging to Brand B, visually overlapping the container.

### 9. Recently Viewed (Conditional)
- **Purpose**: Personalization and recall for returning users.
- **Elements**:
  - **Product Carousel**: Displays items previously interacted with by the user.

### 10. Brand Directory Grid
- **Purpose**: Quick navigation to all available brands.
- **Elements**:
  - **Grid Tiles**: Minimalist blocks containing brand codes/logos and names.

### 11. Newsletter Signup
- **Purpose**: Lead generation and retention.
- **Elements**:
  - **Heading & Copy**: Value prop for subscribing.
  - **Input Field**: Email address entry.
  - **Submit Button**: Action to subscribe.
